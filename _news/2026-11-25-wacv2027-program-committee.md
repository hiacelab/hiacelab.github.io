We are pleased to share that **Kaiser Hamid** from **HiACE Lab, Texas Tech University**, has been selected to serve as a **Program Committee (PC) member** for two workshops at the **IEEE/CVF Winter Conference on Applications of Computer Vision (WACV 2027)**:

- [Human-Centric Open-World Understanding and Reasoning (HOUR)](https://hour-workshop.github.io/wacv2027/)
- [World Action Models for Autonomous Driving (WAM-AD)](https://wam-ad.github.io/)

Through these roles, he will contribute to the scientific peer-review process and support the advancement of research in human-centric computer vision, multimodal reasoning, and autonomous driving.

These appointments reflect HiACE Lab's continued engagement with the international computer vision research community.
